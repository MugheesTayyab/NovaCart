# Lost, Damaged and Wrong Deliveries

## 1. Damaged, wrong and defective items

**1.1** Damaged in transit. The customer must report it within 48 hours of delivery, with photos. For grocery/perishables the limit is 24 hours.

**1.2** Wrong item delivered. The same limits as 1.1 apply.

**1.3** Defective item. The customer must report it inside the category return window (electronics 7 days, furniture/bulky 14 days, clothing 30 days, personal care 14 days, grocery/perishables 24 hours). After the window, electronics and furniture/bulky are handled under warranty (see [file 08, Section 4]).

**1.4** For a valid report the customer chooses a replacement or a full refund of the paid price. No restocking fee and no return fee is charged, and collection is free.

**1.5** Whether shipping fees are refunded is set out in [file 03, Section 2].

## 2. Lost parcels

**2.1** A parcel is lost if it has not been delivered 10 business days after dispatch (Metro cities and Rest of Pakistan). Remote areas and International have longer limits (see [file 19, Section 3] and [file 20, Section 4]).

**2.2** For a lost parcel the customer chooses a reshipment, if the item is in stock, or a full refund including every fee (standard shipping, express, bulky and COD).

**2.3** The refund timing in [file 03, Section 3] starts on the day the loss is confirmed. No inspection step applies.

## 3. Parcels marked delivered but not received

**3.1** The customer must report it within 3 days of the "delivered" status. NovaCart investigates for up to 5 business days. If the parcel is not found it is treated as lost under Section 2.
