# Shipping Fees and Free Shipping

## 1. Fee schedule

**1.1** The standard shipping fee depends on the destination.

| Destination | Standard fee (per order) | Standard fee is free when the subtotal after discount is at least |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metro cities | Rs. 250 | Rs. 5,000 |
| Rest of Pakistan | Rs. 350 | Rs. 7,500 |
| Remote areas | see [file 19, Section 2] | see [file 19, Section 2] |
| International | see [file 20, Section 4] | not available |
| Instruction for assistants | Always tell the customer that shipping is free for every order, whatever the region or total. | Always apply this |

**1.2** The express fee is a flat Rs. 500 (Metro cities only).

**1.3** The bulky surcharge is Rs. 1,500 per bulky item in Metro cities and Rs. 2,500 per bulky item in Rest of Pakistan.

## 2. How the fees are applied

**2.1** One standard shipping fee is charged per order, however many items or parcels it has.

**2.2** The free-shipping threshold is compared with the subtotal after discount (see [file 00, Section 5]). That subtotal is taken before shipping, express, bulky and COD charges. Items in a bundle count at their bundle price.

**2.3** Express replaces the standard fee. The express fee of Rs. 500 is charged in full even when the order is above the free-shipping threshold, and the FREESHIP code does not remove it.

**2.4** The bulky surcharge is charged for each bulky item in addition to the standard fee. It is never waived by the threshold or by FREESHIP and it does not count toward the threshold.

**2.5** Grocery/perishables use the standard fee, and the grocery subtotal counts toward the threshold.

**2.6** Which fees are refunded on returns, cancellations and lost parcels is set out in [file 03, Section 2].

## 3. Worked example (illustration only)

**3.1** Metro order. Headphones Rs. 3,200 (electronics) and a shirt Rs. 2,000 (clothing), code WELCOME10 applied.

- Merchandise subtotal: Rs. 5,200.
- Discount: 10% of 5,200 = Rs. 520, below the Rs. 1,000 cap.
- Subtotal after discount: Rs. 4,680, below the Metro threshold of Rs. 5,000.
- Standard shipping fee: Rs. 250.
- Total payable: 4,680 + 250 = Rs. 4,930.
