# Shipping Fee Update 2027

## 1. What changes

**1.1** From 2027-02-01 the shipping fees for Metro cities and Rest of Pakistan are as follows.

| Destination | Standard fee (per order) | Standard fee is free when the subtotal after discount is at least |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metro cities | Rs. 300 | Rs. 6,000 |
| Rest of Pakistan | Rs. 400 | Rs. 8,500 |

**1.2** The express fee becomes Rs. 600 (Metro cities only).

**1.3** The bulky surcharge becomes Rs. 1,800 per bulky item in Metro cities and Rs. 3,000 per bulky item in Rest of Pakistan.

## 2. What does not change

**2.1** Fees for Remote areas and International orders are unchanged and stay in [file 19] and [file 20]. All other shipping rules stay as in [file 05] and [file 06].
