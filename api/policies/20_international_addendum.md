# International Orders Addendum

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This addendum applies to deliveries to the USA, the UK and the UAE (see [file 00, Section 2]). Where this addendum is silent, the general files apply.

## 2. What can be ordered

**2.1** Electronics, clothing and personal care can be delivered internationally. Grocery/perishables and furniture/bulky items cannot.

## 3. Prices and GST

**3.1** GST is not charged on international orders. The price payable for each item is its listed price divided by 1.18, rounded half up to a whole rupee per item.

**3.2** Discount codes and their minimum amounts are worked out on these export prices.

## 4. Shipping

**4.1** Shipping is a flat fee per order.

| Destination | Shipping fee | Delivery time after dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UAE | Rs. 3,500 | 7 to 10 business days |
| UK | Rs. 5,500 | 10 to 14 business days |
| USA | Rs. 6,500 | 12 to 18 business days |

**4.2** There is no free-shipping threshold, no express delivery and no FREESHIP code for international orders.

**4.3** A parcel is lost if it has not been delivered 25 business days after dispatch (see [file 07, Section 2]).

**4.4** Worked example (illustration only). A UAE order has one electronics item with a listed price of Rs. 11,800 and uses WELCOME10. Export price: 11,800 / 1.18 = Rs. 10,000. The discount is 10% of 10,000 = Rs. 1,000, which equals the cap. Subtotal after discount: Rs. 9,000. Shipping: Rs. 3,500. Total payable: Rs. 12,500.

## 5. Payment

**5.1** International orders are paid by card only. The customer pays any customs duties and import taxes in the destination country.

## 6. Returns

**6.1** The return window is 10 days from delivery for every category that can be shipped internationally.

**6.2** Personal care items cannot be returned from international orders for change of mind. They can be returned for a NovaCart error.

**6.3** The customer arranges and pays the return shipping. No return label fee is charged. Restocking fees in [file 01, Section 3] still apply.

**6.4** Refunds go back to the card 10 business days after the refund approval date, instead of the time in [file 03, Section 3]. Shipping fees are refunded only under [file 03, Section 2].

## 7. Warranty

**7.1** Electronics keep the 12-month warranty in [file 08]. The customer pays shipping to NovaCart and NovaCart pays the return shipping.
