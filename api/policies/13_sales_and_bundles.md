# Sales and Bundle Deals

## 1. Sale prices

**1.1** A sale price already includes the reduction. The sale price is the item's selling price at checkout and in refunds.

**1.2** A sale price counts as the item price in step 1 of [file 00, Section 5].

## 2. Flash sales and Final Sale

**2.1** A flash sale lasts up to 24 hours. Each customer can buy at most 2 units of a flash sale item.

**2.2** Flash sale items can be returned under the normal rules unless they are marked "Final Sale".

**2.3** Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned for change of mind (see [file 04, Section 2]). Warranty still applies where the category has warranty.

## 3. Price changes

**3.1** If an item's price drops after the customer has bought it, NovaCart does not refund the difference.

## 4. Bundle deals

**4.1** A bundle deal is a set of items sold together for one bundle price. Each item in the bundle has a list price shown on the bundle page.

**4.2** Bundle deals cannot be combined with discount codes. Items in a bundle are not eligible for any discount code and do not count toward an eligible subtotal.

**4.3** The bundle price counts toward the merchandise subtotal and toward free-shipping thresholds.

**4.4** If one item of a bundle is returned, its share of the bundle price is the bundle price multiplied by the item's list price and divided by the sum of the list prices of all items in the bundle, rounded half up. If the shares do not add up to the bundle price, the difference goes to the item with the highest list price (the first listed if two are equal).

**4.5** If every item of a bundle is returned, the refund is the full bundle price, before any fees.

**4.6** Each item in a bundle follows the return window and the condition rules of its own category.

**4.7** Worked example (illustration only). A starter bundle costs Rs. 3,333 and contains earphones (list price Rs. 2,500), a charger (list price Rs. 1,000) and a cable (list price Rs. 500). The list prices add up to Rs. 4,000. The shares are earphones 2,083, charger 833 and cable 417, which add up to 3,333.
