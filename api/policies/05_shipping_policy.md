# Shipping Policy

## 1. Scope and dispatch

**1.1** This policy covers orders Sold by NovaCart. Marketplace orders have their own dispatch rules in [file 17, Section 3].

**1.2** NovaCart dispatches an order within 1 business day of payment confirmation. For COD orders the 1 business day starts when the order is verified (see [file 11, Section 3]).

## 2. Delivery times and delivery options

**2.1** Delivery times count in business days from dispatch.

| Destination or option | Delivery time after dispatch |
| --- | --- |
| Metro cities, standard | 2 to 4 business days |
| Metro cities, express | 1 to 2 business days |
| Rest of Pakistan, standard | 4 to 6 business days |
| Bulky items (Metro cities and Rest of Pakistan) | 5 to 8 business days |
| Grocery (Metro cities only) | 1 to 2 business days |
| Remote areas | see [file 19, Section 3] |
| International | see [file 20, Section 4] |

**2.2** Express delivery is available in Metro cities only. It cannot be chosen if the order contains a bulky item. The express fee is in [file 06, Section 2].

**2.3** Bulky items are delivered only to Metro cities and Rest of Pakistan. Someone must be present to receive them.

**2.4** If an express order is delivered more than 2 business days after dispatch, the express fee is refunded as store credit.

**2.5** Grocery/perishables are delivered to Metro cities only. An order containing grocery can only be placed with a Metro city address.

## 3. Tracking and address changes

**3.1** A tracking number is emailed when the order is dispatched.

**3.2** The delivery address can be changed only before dispatch. It cannot be changed after dispatch.

## 4. Failed deliveries

**4.1** The courier makes 3 delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt the order is returned to NovaCart.

**4.2** A prepaid order whose delivery failed is refunded for the merchandise only. Shipping, express, bulky and COD fees are kept. COD orders are cancelled (see [file 11, Section 4]).

## 5. Parcels

**5.1** An order may be split into several parcels that arrive on different days. No extra shipping fee is charged for split parcels.
