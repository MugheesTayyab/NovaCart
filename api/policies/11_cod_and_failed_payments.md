# Cash on Delivery and Failed Payments

## 1. Cash on delivery availability

**1.1** Cash on delivery (COD) is available for Metro cities and Rest of Pakistan. It is not available for Remote areas or International orders.

**1.2** The maximum total payable for a COD order is Rs. 50,000.

## 2. COD fee

**2.1** A COD fee of Rs. 100 is added to each COD order (see [file 00, Section 5]). Whether the fee is refunded is set out in [file 03, Section 2].

## 3. COD verification

**3.1** A COD order with a total payable above Rs. 20,000 is verified by phone. If the customer does not confirm within 24 hours of placing the order, the order is cancelled.

## 4. Refused or failed COD deliveries

**4.1** If delivery fails 3 times, or the customer refuses the parcel, the COD order is cancelled.

**4.2** A second failed or refused COD delivery within 12 months limits the account to prepaid payment methods for 90 days.

## 5. Failed online payments

**5.1** An order with a failed online payment is held for 24 hours and then cancelled automatically.

**5.2** If money was taken but no order was confirmed, or a customer was charged twice, the amount is reversed in the same number of business days as the refund times in [file 03, Section 3]: 7 for cards, 5 for online banking and 2 for wallets.

## 6. COD refunds

**6.1** COD refunds follow [file 03, Section 3].
