# Warranty Policy

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy covers items Sold by NovaCart. Marketplace items have warranty from the seller (see [file 18, Section 4]).

## 2. Warranty periods

**2.1** Warranty periods count from the delivery date.

| Item | Warranty period |
| --- | --- |
| Electronics | 12 months |
| Chargers, cables and other accessories sold separately | 6 months |
| Furniture/bulky | 24 months, structural defects only |
| Clothing, personal care, grocery/perishables | No warranty |

**2.2** An accessory that comes in the box with a device has the same warranty as the device.

## 3. What is covered

**3.1** Warranty covers manufacturing defects that appear during normal use.

**3.2** Warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, repairs by anyone other than NovaCart, normal wear, or cosmetic scratches.

## 4. Remedies

**4.1** NovaCart first tries to repair the item. A repair is completed within 14 calendar days of NovaCart receiving the item (see [file 09, Section 3]).

**4.2** If the item cannot be repaired, NovaCart replaces it with the same or an equivalent model.

**4.3** If no replacement is available, NovaCart refunds the paid price reduced by one equal part for each full month since delivery. The refund is the paid price multiplied by (W minus m) and divided by W, rounded half up, where W is the warranty period in months (12 for electronics, 6 for accessories, 24 for furniture/bulky) and m is the number of full months from the delivery date to the claim date (see [file 00, Section 1]).

**4.4** A replacement item keeps the remaining warranty of the original item. It does not start a new warranty period.

**4.5** An item is repaired at most 2 times. After a third defect of the same kind NovaCart replaces the item or refunds it under 4.3.

## 5. Defects inside the return window

**5.1** A defect reported inside the return window is a NovaCart error return and follows [file 01] and [file 07], not Section 4 of this file.

## 6. Worked example (illustration only)

**6.1** An electronics item with a paid price of Rs. 24,000 was delivered on 2026-01-10. The customer files a claim on 2026-04-12, and no repair or replacement is possible. Full months elapsed: 3. Refund = 24,000 x (12 - 3) / 12 = Rs. 18,000.
