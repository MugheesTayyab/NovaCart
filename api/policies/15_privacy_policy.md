# Privacy Policy

## 1. Who can have an account

**1.1** NovaCart accounts are for people aged 18 or over.

## 2. Data NovaCart collects

**2.1** NovaCart collects account details (name, email, phone number, delivery addresses), order history, payment tokens issued by the payment processor, and technical data such as device type and IP address.

## 3. How data is used

**3.1** Data is used to process orders, deliver parcels, handle returns and warranty claims, prevent fraud and improve the service.

**3.2** Marketing emails are sent only to customers who have agreed to them. A customer can opt out at any time (see [file 16, Section 5]).

## 4. Sharing data

**4.1** NovaCart shares delivery details with couriers and payment details with payment processors.

**4.2** For marketplace orders NovaCart shares the customer's name, delivery address and phone number with the seller. No other personal data is shared with sellers.

**4.3** NovaCart does not sell personal data.

## 5. Cookies

**5.1** Essential cookies are always on. Analytics cookies are optional and the customer can switch them off in the cookie settings.

## 6. Security

**6.1** An account is locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts.

**6.2** NovaCart tells affected customers about a data breach within 72 hours of confirming it.

## 7. Contact

**7.1** Privacy questions go to privacy@novacart.example.
