# Non-Returnable Items

## 1. Items that cannot be returned for change of mind

**1.1** Grocery/perishables. Every item in this category is non-returnable for change of mind.

**1.2** Opened personal care items. A personal care item whose seal has been broken cannot be returned. A sealed personal care item can be returned within 14 days (see [file 01, Section 2]).

**1.3** Innerwear and swimwear. These clothing items cannot be returned once the hygiene seal has been removed. With the seal intact they follow the normal clothing window.

## 2. Items that cannot be returned because of how they were sold

**2.1** Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase cannot be returned for change of mind. Warranty still applies where the category has warranty (see [file 08, Section 2]).

**2.2** Furniture/bulky items marked "Custom" or made to order cannot be returned for change of mind.

## 3. Exceptions

**3.1** Any item can be returned in any category, including the items in Sections 1 and 2, if it is a NovaCart error and it is reported inside the limits in [file 07, Section 1].

**3.2** The return fees and restocking fees in [file 01] do not apply to returns under 3.1.

## 4. Marketplace items

**4.1** Marketplace sellers must follow the same list of non-returnable items (see [file 18, Section 1]).
