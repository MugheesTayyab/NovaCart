# Store Credit

## 1. What store credit is

**1.1** Store credit is a balance held on the customer's NovaCart account. Rs. 1 of credit is worth Rs. 1.

## 2. How credit is issued

**2.1** Credit is issued when a customer chooses store credit for a refund, when NovaCart refunds a late express fee (see [file 05, Section 2.4]) and when NovaCart gives goodwill credit.

## 3. Store credit bonus

**3.1** A customer who chooses store credit instead of a refund to the original payment method receives a bonus of 5% of the refund amount, rounded half up, up to a maximum of Rs. 1,000 per refund.

**3.2** There is no bonus on credit that can only be issued as store credit, such as a late express fee refund or the part of an order that was paid with store credit.

**3.3** The bonus expires together with the credit it was issued with.

## 4. Expiry

**4.1** Credit expires 12 months after the day it is issued, on the same calendar date. Unused credit is lost on expiry and the expiry date cannot be extended.

## 5. Using store credit

**5.1** Credit can be used at checkout for all or part of an order, together with other payment methods.

**5.2** Credit cannot be used to buy items from marketplace sellers (see [file 17, Section 6]).

**5.3** Credit cannot be withdrawn as cash and cannot be transferred to another account.

## 6. Orders paid partly with store credit

**6.1** A refund for an order paid partly with store credit goes first to the other payment method, up to the amount that method paid. Any remainder is refunded to store credit.

**6.2** Worked example (illustration only). A customer chooses store credit for a refund of Rs. 8,000. The bonus is 5% of 8,000 = Rs. 400, so Rs. 8,400 of credit is issued.
