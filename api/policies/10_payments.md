# Payments

## 1. Accepted payment methods

**1.1** For delivery inside Pakistan NovaCart accepts debit and credit cards (Visa and Mastercard), online banking, wallets (JazzCash and Easypaisa), cash on delivery (see [file 11]) and store credit (see [file 14]).

**1.2** International orders can be paid by card only (see [file 20, Section 5]).

## 2. Security

**2.1** Card payments above Rs. 20,000 need 3-D Secure verification.

**2.2** NovaCart does not store full card numbers. Card details are held by the payment processor.

## 3. Order limits

**3.1** The maximum total payable for a card, online banking or wallet order is Rs. 250,000.

**3.2** The maximum total payable for a cash on delivery order is Rs. 40,000.

## 4. When payment is taken

**4.1** Card, online banking and wallet payments are taken at checkout. Cash on delivery is paid to the courier at delivery.

## 5. Currency and receipts

**5.1** All payments are charged in PKR. If a card is issued in another currency, the card issuer sets the exchange rate and any conversion fee. NovaCart does not refund conversion fees.

**5.2** An email invoice is sent within 1 business day of payment confirmation.
