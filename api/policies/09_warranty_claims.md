# How to Make a Warranty Claim

## 1. Making a claim

**1.1** The customer opens the warranty portal and submits the order number, a description of the defect and photos.

**1.2** NovaCart decides whether the claim is accepted within 3 business days of receiving the claim.

## 2. Collection and costs

**2.1** For an accepted claim NovaCart arranges courier collection and return of the item at no cost to the customer.

**2.2** If the claim is rejected because the defect is not covered (see [file 08, Section 3]), the customer pays an inspection and return fee of Rs. 400 before the item is sent back.

## 3. Repair time

**3.1** NovaCart completes a repair within 14 calendar days of receiving the item. If it takes longer, the customer can ask for a replacement instead.

## 4. Customer responsibilities

**4.1** The customer is responsible for backing up and removing personal data from a device before sending it. NovaCart is not responsible for data lost during repair.

## 5. Appeals

**5.1** A rejected claim can be appealed within 7 days of the decision. NovaCart answers the appeal within 5 business days.
