# Seller Return Obligations

## 1. Return windows and conditions

**1.1** Marketplace items follow the return windows, conditions and fees in [file 01], and the list of non-returnable items in [file 04]. A seller cannot set a shorter window or add fees.

## 2. Confirming a return

**2.1** The seller must confirm receipt of a returned item within 3 business days of NovaCart's courier delivering it to the seller. For marketplace items this replaces the 2-business-day inspection in [file 01, Section 6]. If the seller does not confirm in time, NovaCart approves the refund on the third business day.

**2.2** The refund approval date is the day the seller confirms, or the third business day if the seller does not confirm.

## 3. Who pays for the return

**3.1** The seller pays the return shipping for a NovaCart error return. The customer pays the return label fee for a change-of-mind return, as set out in [file 01, Section 4].

## 4. Warranty on marketplace items

**4.1** The seller gives the warranty on marketplace items. For electronics the seller's warranty is at least 6 months from delivery. The NovaCart warranty in [file 08] does not apply.

## 5. Refused returns

**5.1** If a seller refuses a valid return, NovaCart refunds the customer and charges the seller a penalty of Rs. 1,000 for each refused return.

## 6. Disputes

**6.1** NovaCart decides a dispute between a customer and a seller within 5 business days.
