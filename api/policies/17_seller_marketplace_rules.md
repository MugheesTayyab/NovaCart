# Seller Marketplace Rules

## 1. Who can sell

**1.1** A seller must be a registered business with a CNIC or NTN and a bank account in the business name. Marketplace items are shown as "Sold by" followed by the seller's name.

## 2. Commission and payout

**2.1** NovaCart charges a commission on the selling price of each item, by category.

| Category | Commission |
| --- | --- |
| Electronics | 6% |
| Clothing | 12% |
| Furniture/bulky | 10% |
| Personal care | 10% |
| Grocery/perishables | 5% |

**2.2** NovaCart also charges a handling fee of Rs. 50 per order item line.

**2.3** The seller payout for an item is the selling price minus the commission (rounded half up) minus the handling fee. Example: a clothing item sold for Rs. 3,000 has a commission of Rs. 360 and a payout of 3,000 - 360 - 50 = Rs. 2,590.

**2.4** Payouts are made every Wednesday for items delivered at least 7 days earlier that are not in a return or dispute.

## 3. Dispatch

**3.1** A seller must dispatch an order within 2 business days of payment confirmation.

## 4. Seller performance

**4.1** A seller whose cancellation rate is above 5% in a rolling 30 days gets a warning. Above 10% the seller is suspended for 30 days.

## 5. Prohibited items

**5.1** Sellers must not list counterfeit goods, items that are illegal to sell in Pakistan, or items that NovaCart has banned.

## 6. Rules that affect customers

**6.1** Marketplace items follow the return windows of [file 01] and the extra rules in [file 18].

**6.2** Store credit cannot be used for marketplace items, and promo codes do not apply to them.
