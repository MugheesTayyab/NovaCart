# Remote Areas Addendum

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This addendum applies to deliveries to Remote areas: Azad Jammu & Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan (see [file 00, Section 2]). Where this addendum is silent, the general files apply.

## 2. Shipping fees

**2.1** The standard shipping fee is Rs. 600 per order. It is free when the subtotal after discount is at least Rs. 12,000.

**2.2** Express delivery, bulky items and grocery/perishables are not available for Remote areas. Cash on delivery is not available for Remote areas.

**2.3** The FREESHIP code is not valid for Remote areas.

## 3. Delivery times

**3.1** Delivery takes 7 to 10 business days after dispatch.

**3.2** A parcel is lost if it has not been delivered 15 business days after dispatch (see [file 07, Section 2]).

## 4. Returns

**4.1** Return windows in Remote areas are 3 days longer than the windows in [file 01, Section 2]: electronics 10 days, clothing 33 days, personal care 17 days (sealed items only).

**4.2** The return label fee is Rs. 400. All other return rules follow [file 01].

## 5. Warranty

**5.1** A warranty repair in Remote areas is completed within 21 calendar days of NovaCart receiving the item, instead of 14.
